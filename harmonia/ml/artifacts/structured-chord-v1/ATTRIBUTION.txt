Harmonia experimental structured chord model E004-transposition-tcn

Trained using GuitarSet 1.1.0 by Qingyang Xi, Rachel M. Bittner,
Johan Pauwels, Xuzhou Ye and Juan P. Bello.
Source: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3371780
Dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Transformations: microphone audio decoding/resampling, chroma/bass/energy/flux
features, reduction of performed chord labels to component targets,
feature-space pitch augmentation, supervised neural training, ONNX export.
Original audio and annotations are not included. Dataset authors do not
endorse Harmonia. This experimental model has substantial accuracy limits;
see docs/ml-model.md and docs/evaluation.md.
